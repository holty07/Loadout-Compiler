# M7 — ship

Scope: store listings, screenshots, privacy policy, monetisation
implementation, analytics, crash reporting, localisation extraction, beta
channel.

## Exit criterion

The ship checklist in `docs/plan.md` (## Ship checklist), fully ticked:
monetisation implemented as free at launch with no ads and no IAP (revisit
only once there is retention data from real players), developer accounts/certificates/signing
in CI, store listings with screenshots from real runs, privacy policy and
data-safety declarations matching what analytics actually collects, an
analytics taxonomy defined before events are added (including choke
attribution in the wild — the highest-value item on the list), crash
reporting with sim version and seed attached to every report, verified
localisation extraction, accessibility (colour-blind safe palette, text
scaling, reduced motion), a tested device matrix, cold start under three
seconds on the floor device, offline play with graceful daily-seed
degradation, a closed beta run through TestFlight and Google Play internal
testing, a support contact and crash-to-support path, and a post-launch
data-only balance patch proven end to end against beta.
